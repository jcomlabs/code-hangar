## Fixture set F: Safe Manage recommendation enrichment

Contains synthetic, non-sensitive assessment/context combinations for:

- a confident deterministic **Keep** result that AI agrees with;
- a confident **Keep** result that AI changes to **Archive** after selected
  evidence shows substantial but dormant work;
- an ambiguous **Review** result that AI changes to a typed recommendation;
- an archive or removal candidate that AI conservatively changes to **Review**;
- a deterministic **Do not touch** result for which AI enrichment is refused;
- stale evidence for which prior selectors are refused;
- valid and malformed provider action/confidence sections; and
- selected README, manifest, central-file and linked-session excerpts containing
  paths, secret markers and Protected content for redaction/block tests.

Purpose:

- prove that Connector AI adds recommendation value beyond explanation;
- prove that deterministic output remains visible and unchanged;
- prove fail-closed typed parsing and evidence-revision binding;
- prove that no AI result records a decision or reaches mutation; and
- prove that Local fixtures and artifacts contain no Connector trace.
